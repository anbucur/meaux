01 // DISCIPLINE · ATELIER MEAUX

Photography

“The camera does not record light; it carves form out of shadow.”

Anouk Study Photographic Direction

The Archaeology of Light

Photography at Meaux is not passive observation; it is an architectural excavation of human presence. Stripping away superficial ornamentation, we operate in deep, high-contrast monochrome and directional raking light. Every shadow is deliberate—sculpted to accentuate bone structure, posture, and the palpable tension between the subject and the surrounding void.

Sculptural Portraiture & High Fashion

Having photographed campaigns and editorial stories for houses such as Gucci Beauty, Vanderwilt Amsterdam, and Jacquemus, our visual language draws heavily from classical chiaroscuro, modern brutalism, and avant-garde couture. We treat the human body as an immutable monument in space.

Surgical Precision

Every frame is treated as a museum monograph. From medium format digital sensor calibration to custom black-and-white tonal curves, each capture delivers immense depth, velvet blacks, and silvery highlights that command quiet awe.

Photographic Service Scope

Executed with complete creative authorship, from initial mood architecture to final exhibition-grade mastering.

02 // DISCIPLINE · ATELIER MEAUX

Styling

“Garments are not adornment; they are body architecture.”

Vanderwilt Amsterdam Silhouette & Leather Direction

Silhouette Architecture

Styling at Meaux is rooted in tactile contrast: heavy oil-tanned lambskins paired with flowing Japanese cottons; structured shoulders framing vulnerability; razor-sharp hemlines cutting through space. We do not dress bodies—we construct living silhouettes that hold sovereign presence in any room.

Archival Curation & Atelier Collaborations

Working closely with independent artisan houses like Vanderwilt Amsterdam as well as archival collections from Jean Paul Gaultier, Rick Owens, and Jacquemus, our wardrobe curation favors timeless garments with sculptural weight over ephemeral trends.

Movement & Sensory Weight

How a textile moves in air, how leather creases under natural light, how a collarbone catches an edge of darkness—these tactile details determine how the garment lives on film and in memory.

Styling Service Scope

04 // DISCIPLINE · ATELIER MEAUX

Interior Design

“A room is not a container; it is an emotional atmosphere shaped by material and shadow.”

Spatial Atmosphere Study Light & Shadow Choreography

The Architecture of Feeling

Space is the physical extension of consciousness. Meaux's interior design practice is founded on atmospheric tension: balancing raw, brutalist architectural textures with deep, sensual softness. We reject sterile minimalism in favor of visceral environments that calm the mind and envelope the senses.

Material Purity & Tactility

We compose rooms using authentic, tactile elements: midnight lambskin, liquid chrome, dark oiled European walnut, hand-troweled lime plaster, and raw basalt stone. These materials do not merely age—they patinate, recording the passage of light and life.

Light as the Final Material

Natural daylight is treated as a sculptural blade. By orchestrating directional raking daylight, low-voltage grazing luminaires, and warm ambient pools of darkness, our spaces transform continually throughout the day from contemplative sanctuary to dramatic nighttime lounge.

The Tactile Material Palette

Every space designed by Meaux is built from a deliberate balance of temperature, grain, and reflection.

Obsidian Lambskin

Supple, non-reflective leather absorbing sound and light, providing intimate sensory comfort.

Liquid Chrome

Mirror-polished cold metal accents reflecting shifting shadows and architectural lines.

Raw Basalt & Tadelakt

Velvety mineral plasters and porous natural stone imparting timeless thermal stability.

Smoked Oiled Walnut

Deep rich grain with warm undertones, grounding the room in organic quietude.

The Creative Behind Meaux

Mohammed

“Soft form. Sharp instinct. Form follows feeling.”

I founded Meaux out of an obsession with how image, silhouette, and space interact to produce emotion. Operating from Amsterdam and commissioning worldwide, my practice lives at the intersection where photography, fashion styling, digital curation, and spatial design collapse into a single coherent universe.

I believe true luxury is not excessive ornamentation, but radical restraint. It is knowing exactly when to hold back and when to strike with an uncompromising visual gesture. Trained by raw instinct rather than rigid dogma, I treat every project—whether an intimate portrait, a fashion lookbook, an entire brand ecosystem, or an architectural sanctuary—as an emotional atmosphere.

Why practice all four disciplines simultaneously? Because they are not separate silos—they are different scales of the exact same question: How does it feel to inhabit this world? When the same creative eye choreographs the room, styles the clothes, directs the photograph, and curates the feed, the result is an unmistakable, unbreakable world.
